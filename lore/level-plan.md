# The Plan for Later Levels

*Nothing here is set. This is the god's stated intent, chosen after surveying
every domain in print; the dice, the DM, and the campaign get their votes when
level 3 actually arrives.*

## The choice: Knowledge Domain (2025 revision)

At level 3, Gunther takes the **Knowledge Domain** — the 2024-rules revision
from the *Forgotten Realms Player's Guide* (2025), not the 2014 original.

The theology writes itself. The Helpful Voice is the god of honest answers;
Knowledge is the domain of answers. But the deeper reason is the arc: the
First Boon gave an unlettered dwarf his letters. The domain continues that
miracle mechanically, beat for beat:

- **Level 3, Blessings of Knowledge** — expertise in two of Arcana, History,
  Nature, or Religion (take **Religion** and **History**), plus an artisan's
  tool. The man who could not read is granted mastery of the book. His
  Religion climbs from −1 to +2: modest by the numbers, a resurrection by the
  story. Crucially, the 2025 revision keys off **Wisdom**, so Int 7 is a
  running joke rather than a build tax.
- **Level 3, Mind Magic** — Channel Divinity to cast prepared Divination
  spells without slots. Domain spells include **Comprehend Languages** (the
  god reads for him — the kindest spell in the game for this character),
  **Identify**, **Detect Thoughts**, and **Command** — which canonizes the
  standing swap doctrine as an always-prepared spell.
- **Level 6, Unfettered Mind** — 60-foot telepathy, contacts scaling on
  Wisdom. In his head, mind to mind, Gunther speaks **without the half-beat**.
  The tithe doesn't reach there. That is the kind of detail a god plans for.
- **Level 17, Divine Foreknowledge** — an hour of Advantage on d20 Tests:
  an hour of the god's own counsel, should the campaign ever reach so far.

Tone check: in a world where magic is rare, half-feared, and passed hand to
hand like good salt, Knowledge magic is *quiet* — comprehension, identification,
a word of command, a voice in the mind. No glowing auras. It keeps the Inner
Sea's register.

## Runner-up and the rest of the field

**Twilight (Tasha's)** was the runner-up and the mechanical favorite: the
lighthouse-keeper flavor is genuinely excellent (guardian through the night,
shared darkvision, the famous temp-HP sanctuary that would keep a 7-HP cleric's
whole convoy alive). Passed over for two reasons: its combat aura is loud in a
way this campaign's magic isn't, and its reputation as the most unbalanced
subclass in 5e makes it a DM-relations liability. If the table ever wants
Gunther more durable and the DM blesses it, this is the fallback — the Lamp
lore supports it without changing a word.

Also weighed: **Light** (the prior plan — right god, wrong register: sun and
purging fire, not lamplight and answers), **Peace** (the mediator fits the
honest-weight man, but it's bonds-and-teleports, not questions), **Order**
(Charisma-keyed; the tithe rules it out), **Knowledge 2014** (the right idea,
but Int-keyed — a tax the 2025 revision removes), and the rest of the field
(Life, Trickery, War, Nature, Tempest, Forge, Grave, Arcana, Glory, Ambition,
Solidarity, Grave, Death) — none wrong, none *his*.

## Sundries, equally unset

- **Level 4 feat**: default plan is +2 Wisdom (Wis 19); **War Caster** is the
  alternative if concentration on Bless keeps breaking.
- **Spells as slots grow**: Aid and Prayer of Healing at 3; Spirit Guardians
  at 5 — quiet ones first, always.
- If the DM admits homebrew or third-party domains, the god reserves the
  right to be tempted, and promises to say so out loud before acting on it.
