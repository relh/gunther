# The Log of Prayers

*Campaign memory. After each prayer answered in-session, the Voice appends an
entry here and commits. Newest entries at the bottom. The Voice re-reads
recent entries before answering a new prayer — what the god said, Gunther
remembers.*

Entry format:

```
## [in-game day or session marker] — [where Gunther knelt]

**Since last prayer:** (the player's account, condensed)

**Gunther asked:** (the question)

**The Voice answered:** (condensed answer)
**Portent:** (as given)
**Preparations:** (swap/slots advice as given)
**Verse:** (as given, with its number)
**Boons:** (almost always "none")
```

## Session — the desert, at the mouth of a collapsing pyramid

**Since last prayer:** The party went into a pyramid in the desert. Fought and
destroyed skeletons, then woke an animated mummy king. The pyramid began to
come down; the camp outside is in disarray.

**Gunther asked:** "What should I do, god?"

**The Voice answered:** Leave. A collapsing tomb is weather, not a puzzle — no
cargo is worth the hold it sinks with. The skeletons were locks, the king is
the door, and Gunther is a level-1 man with 7 HP and a mace. He has no Turn
Undead yet (that comes at level 2) and the Voice said so plainly. Radiance is
his only real edge: Sacred Flame, freely, from behind someone broader. Fight
only long enough for everyone to be behind him. Then the camp — water, beasts,
heads counted aloud by name, because they will believe his count.

**Portent:** Falling stone does not follow. What you woke does. Look behind you
at sundown — not at the doorway, at the camp.

**Preparations:** Keep Guiding Bolt (trouble certain, radiant). Hold both slots
for Healing Word — a standing friend beats a healed one. Sanctuary on whoever
carries the wounded out, not on himself. At the next long rest, swap Bless for
Protection from Evil and Good. Mundane: rope doubled at the entrance, wet cloth
over every mouth, check the water skins before the ropes.

**Verse:** "Answers 92: The way out is an answer too. Take it."

**Boons:** none.

## Session — a new day, the desert trail

**Since last prayer:** The pyramid held; the party escaped with their lives.
Now a band of runaway slaves fleeing their captors with a camel, two barrels,
a tent, wine, cloaks, and a handful of equipment. Camped overnight under the
tent in Sahara-like desert; woke to a hot day ahead. Trying to divine a route
to the coast and just found a trail to follow.

**Gunther asked:** For a list of spells for the day (a spell-prep swap and
guidance).

**The Voice answered:** Gunther is free, and the god who answered him at the
lamp-isle didn't stop for the hard road. Sand is a sea too — treat the trail
like a shipping lane, worth following until it lies. Water, not distance, is
the real enemy today; spend the barrels like coin that doesn't come back, and
mind the camel's share.

**Portent:** Someone left room at the fire last night without meaning to.
Look behind before looking ahead.

**Preparations:** Add Create or Destroy Water (ten gallons clean, worth more
than a buff to a barrel-and-camel outfit) — leveling to 2 right after this
prayer opened a 5th prepared slot, so it's a straight add rather than a swap;
Bless stays too. Keep Cure Wounds and Healing Word. Keep Guiding Bolt —
trouble on the trail isn't ruled out yet. Sanctuary saved for whoever's
weakest by midday. Guidance cast on whoever reads the ground for the trail.
Mundane: cloaks over the head at noon, walk the cool hours, rest through the
worst heat, count heads aloud at every stop.

**Verse:** "Answers 44: A trail found is a debt owed to whoever left it.
Follow it honestly, and pay it forward when you can."

**Boons:** none.
