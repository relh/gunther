## Session 1 — the pyramid and the black mark

**Play date:** not recorded. Reconstructed on 2026-10-15 from the
[California St recap](transcripts/2026-10-15-california-st.md), the
[prayer log](../prayers/log.md), and the player's follow-up clarification.
The memo mostly recounts Session 2; the player confirmed the session split
and the branding described below.

We went into the pyramid. The earlier prayer log records skeletons destroyed,
an animated mummy king awakened, and fears that the pyramid was coming down.
We also encountered a spooky ghost revenant that branded every one of us:
**a black mark on the back of the neck**. What the marks mean, and what they
might do, remain unknown.

We escaped the pyramid alive. The prayer log's next entry confirms that the
pyramid held. The recap recalls two people going down into the pyramid while
others went to the camp; the precise sequence of the party splitting up is
not clear enough to reconstruct further.

**Where we stopped:** the escape from the pyramid; Session 2 picks up with
our return to the collapsing slave camp.

**Open thread:** everyone bears the revenant's black mark. No effect or
resolution has yet been recorded.

## Session 2 — flight into the desert

**Play date:** not recorded. Reconstructed on 2026-10-15, primarily from the
[California St voice memo](transcripts/2026-10-15-california-st.md), with the
player's clarification and earlier prayer entries identified where used.
The transcript is automatic and some passages are unclear.

### Out of the slave camp

After escaping the pyramid, we returned to the collapsing slave camp and
fled into the desert. We secured a camel and loaded it with barrels and
supplies. The recap also mentions weapons looted from the pyramid, but does
not clearly identify them. We were runaway slaves, trying to get away from
our captors and reach the coast.

### East, toward the water

We chose to head east, believing the water lay that way. The camel somehow
helped with directions, but the recap does not establish a single clear
instruction from it. We walked through the rest of a fiercely hot day.

We made a tent from a sheet and slept that night, with a watch kept by
party members who needed little sleep. Sleeping in the desert did not
give us a proper long rest. To avoid the heat, we then shifted to resting
by day and travelling at night. By the end of the following night's march,
some of us were reportedly suffering exhaustion; the affected characters
and exact levels were not recorded.

### Water and the cost of keeping moving

We had two barrels: one held wine, and the other had carried supplies or
rations, which the discussion says were moved into a pack. Water was a
constant worry, both for us and for the camel.

Gunther prayed to the Helpful Voice and produced water. The discussion
also mentions the ability to produce ten gallons of clean water; the table
discussed roughly twenty gallons between us. The exact casts, contributors,
and remaining supply need confirmation. Magic eased the journey, but spell
slots still limited how much help we could call on.

The earlier prayer log places Gunther's advance to level 2 on the desert
trail: 15 maximum HP, a third first-level spell slot, Divine Spark, and
Create or Destroy Water added to his preparations. These are earlier
recorded changes, not new rewards from this recap. The spoken description
of water as a "boon" does not establish an additional permanent boon.

### Where we stopped — trouble at camp

After a night's walking, we were setting up camp and preparing to sleep
when a squad appeared who looked like fellow escapees. The recap initially
calls this an attack, then corrects itself: the fight was about to happen.
No combat result is recorded.

The latest prayer entry describes the impending encounter as **four of the
party's five members facing eight bandits**. That count comes from the
prayer log; the memo adds their apparent resemblance to fellow escapees.
Their identities and motives remain unconfirmed.

**Next scene:** the confrontation at the desert camp, before resting.

- The DM described a travel-rest system in which this exposed flight through
  the desert allows short rests but does not provide normal long rests.
  The DM also said the system was not fully clarified yet; record the final
  ruling when it is settled.
- The player confirmed two sessions: the pyramid escape, then the flight
  from the slave camp into the desert. Their play dates remain unrecorded.
- Confirm who has exhaustion, its levels, and current HP and spell slots.
- Confirm the water supply, ration count, and equipment after the escape.
  The memo's early equipment passage is too garbled to establish an inventory.
- The table asked whether absent players' characters consume water. No
  answer is captured in the recording.
