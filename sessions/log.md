We went into the pyramid. The earlier prayer log records skeletons destroyed,

**Where we stopped:** the escape from the pyramid; Session 2 picks up with
our return to the collapsing slave camp.

## Session 2 — flight into the desert

**Play date:** not recorded. Reconstructed on 2026-10-15, primarily from the
[California St voice memo](transcripts/2026-10-15-california-st.md), with the
player's clarification and earlier prayer entries identified where used.
The transcript is automatic and some passages are unclear.

### Out of the slave camp

After escaping the pyramid, we returned to the collapsing slave camp and
fled into the desert. We secured a camel and loaded it with barrels and
supplies. The recap also mentions weapons looted from the pyramid, but does
not clearly identify them. We were runaway slaves, trying to get away from
our captors and reach the coast.

### East, toward the water

We chose to head east, believing the water lay that way. The camel somehow
helped with directions, but the recap does not establish a single clear
instruction from it. We walked through the rest of a fiercely hot day.

We made a tent from a sheet and slept that night, with a watch kept by
party members who needed little sleep. Sleeping in the desert did not
give us a proper long rest. To avoid the heat, we then shifted to resting
by day and travelling at night. By the end of the following night's march,
some of us were reportedly suffering exhaustion; the affected characters
and exact levels were not recorded.

### Water and the cost of keeping moving

We had two barrels: one held wine, and the other had carried supplies or
rations, which the discussion says were moved into a pack. Water was a
constant worry, both for us and for the camel.

Gunther prayed to the Helpful Voice and produced water. The discussion
also mentions the ability to produce ten gallons of clean water; the table
discussed roughly twenty gallons between us. The exact casts, contributors,
and remaining supply need confirmation. Magic eased the journey, but spell
slots still limited how much help we could call on.

The earlier prayer log places Gunther's advance to level 2 on the desert
trail: 15 maximum HP, a third first-level spell slot, Divine Spark, and
Create or Destroy Water added to his preparations. These are earlier
recorded changes, not new rewards from this recap. The spoken description
of water as a "boon" does not establish an additional permanent boon.

### Where we stopped — trouble at camp

After a night's walking, we were setting up camp and preparing to sleep
when a squad appeared who looked like fellow escapees. The recap initially
calls this an attack, then corrects itself: the fight was about to happen.
No combat result is recorded.

The latest prayer entry describes the impending encounter as **four of the
party's five members facing eight bandits**. That count comes from the
prayer log; the memo adds their apparent resemblance to fellow escapees.
Their identities and motives remain unconfirmed.

**Next scene:** the confrontation at the desert camp, before resting.

## 2026-10-15 — the fortified oasis and the caravan

**Source:** [California St 2 — edited campaign transcript](transcripts/2026-10-15-california-st-2.md),
recorded during play. Session number is unconfirmed. The recording begins
mid-discussion; it does not cover the resolution of the encounter at the end
of Session 2.

### A way to feed nine travelers

Nine of us were travelling toward a fortified oasis, short of food and water.
We discussed ambushing a supply caravan for provisions and equipment. Passing
ourselves off as soldiers or slipping into the fort with a caravan sounded
risky while we still looked like escaped slaves.

Prim sent her hawk familiar ahead. The oasis was about eight hours away at
our pace, with several paths through its palisade. Nearby dunes offered cover
and views of individual approaches, but no position overlooked every entrance.
The hawk also spotted movement northeast of the oasis and went to investigate.

We rested through the heat beneath our sheet tent, propped up on spears.
Elementalism provided some cooling air and water spray, but the sandy,
uncomfortable shelter earned only a short rest. Our fellow escapees were
uneasy about approaching soldiers; a carefully chosen caravan seemed a more
manageable target than the garrison.

### Two armies near the water

Prim recalled that an empire and a confederation of desert tribes had been
at war for about a decade. We appeared to be near the front line of a bloody
stalemate. Oases were fought over for water and as bases for troops and
supplies. The damaged oasis ahead appeared to be occupied and fortified by
the imperial side. The faction names need confirmation from the DM; the
automatic transcript is inconsistent.

The hawk returned with news of a larger tribal camp northeast of the oasis,
roughly four or five hours' walk from it. It held more soldiers and many
camels, with less fortification and more effort to stay hidden. We considered
setting the armies against each other, but did not carry out that plan.
The hawk also recalled movement to the south, possibly a caravan.

By evening we had finished the water and the remaining wine. Both barrels
were empty, and the camel was beginning to thirst. Gunther kept his final
spell slot in reserve. We headed east, favoring a southern approach to the
oasis and hoping to intercept the moving group.

### The dead in the dunes

A traveler stumbled over a dried corpse. Looking around revealed an old
battlefield: bodies half-buried in the sand, wrecked carts, and dead camels.
The useful equipment had already been taken. We could tell two sides had
fought here, but not who had won or exactly how long ago.

Remembering the pyramid, we crossed cautiously and quietly. None of the
bodies moved. Farther on, there seemed to be faint lights back toward the
battlefield, but nothing clear remained when we looked directly.

### Ten carts, at least twelve guards

From a tall dune we finally saw the oasis, its circular palisade, and guarded
checkpoints on the approaches. Prim changed her familiar into an owl with
the DM's permission and sent it to examine the southern movement. This time
it confirmed a caravan: **roughly ten camel-drawn carts and at least twelve
armed guards**.

We favored intercepting it before it reached the garrison's protection.
Some companions doubted that nine hungry, poorly armored escapees could win
that fight. Gunther tried to intimidate a doubter into confidence, rolled a
one, and managed to hit himself in the face and fall over. The attempt drew
amusement rather than fear. Another attempt at persuasion was proposed.

**Where we stopped:** outside the fortified oasis at night, still debating
the caravan ambush. No attack or captured loot is recorded. Gunther still
has **one first-level spell slot**; his proposed final Create Water was not
cast in this recording.

## 2026-10-15 — seven bolting camels and an offer of food

**Source:** [California St 3 — edited campaign transcript](transcripts/2026-10-15-california-st-3.md).
Continuation of the caravan approach; session number remains unconfirmed.

### Everything we had left

We went toward the caravan rather than waiting for it to reach the oasis.
Gunther raised his hands to the Helpful Voice and made the last water his
magic could supply. We drank and finished the remaining 27 rations. After
roughly thirteen hours of marching, exhaustion was catching up with everyone.

Some of us wondered whether we should join the caravan instead of robbing it.
The DM clarified that the pyramid's slave camp had not been directly run by
the empire. These guards were not necessarily the people we had escaped.
Still, none of us wanted to risk becoming slaves again.

### Wolves that were not there

We settled on a trick: Prim would warn the camels of wolves using Speak with
Animals, and Gunther would supply the howl with Thaumaturgy. If the caravan
split and the guards chased their animals, we might take supplies without
having to defeat them all. Even one loaded camel would be enough to help.

We gathered on a ten-foot rocky ledge near the rear of the caravan. Up close,
the guards looked rested, alert, and well armored. One spotted us and shouted
before we could spring the ambush. We rolled initiative without the surprise
we had hoped for.

Prim warned the camels, and her owl swooped and screeched. A companion tried
Mind Sliver on the guard who had raised the alarm, but he resisted it. The
veteran leading the caravan advanced with a sword and demanded that we put
down our weapons and come down. His guards formed up between us and the carts.

Then Gunther made his wolf howl from behind the rise. **Seven of twelve camels
failed their saves and bolted in different directions.** Some guards gave
chase. Gunther called out that wolves were pursuing us: we were lost souls
in the desert, not a threat. His Deception check came to **16**, after the
Charisma and exhaustion penalties.

### A meal instead of a battle

It was morning by then, and the veteran doubted the story about wolves. But
he could see how desperate we were. He offered food if we helped defend
against the supposed wolves or recover the camels. We would have to give up
our weapons.

Our companions urged us to accept. Natho, Cella, and Callus—the names as
transcribed, with spellings still unconfirmed—put their weapons down. Gunther
was ready to abandon the fight, and the group began descending. Prim remained
on the ledge a moment longer, then reluctantly came down as well.

**Where we stopped:** the ambush has been abandoned. Everyone has come down
from the ledge to deal with the caravan guards, who have offered food on
condition of cooperation and surrendering weapons. No successful damaging
attack or casualty is recorded.

**Changes and open threads:**

- Gunther's separately confirmed **Exhaustion 1** remains the recorded total.
  The group exhaustion discussion does not establish a second level for him.
- Thaumaturgy's wolf howl used no spell slot. Prim's Speak with Animals and

- The veteran demanded weapons, and several companions put theirs down.
