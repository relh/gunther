## 00:00–04:00 — the last provisions and the march

The party decides to approach the caravan, roughly four or five hours away,
rather than wait for it to reach the fortified oasis. Gunther spends his last
spell slot on Create Water. The nine travelers drink and finish the remaining
27 rations, knowing they will arrive with no supplies left.

They continue marching. The DM describes a journey of roughly thirteen hours
and applies exhaustion to the remaining travelers, noting that some were
already exhausted. Gunther's exhaustion was recorded separately by the player
as level 1; this passage does not clearly establish another increase for him.

Some party members begin to favor joining the caravan over attacking it. The
DM clarifies that the slave camp at the pyramid was not directly run by the
empire, so the caravan's people are not automatically their former captors.
Other characters remain adamant that they will not accept slavery again.

## 04:00–13:00 — a plan to scatter the camels

As the caravan becomes audible, the party discusses using ranged cantrips and
frightening the camels to split the guards. Prim will use Speak with Animals
to warn of wolves, while Gunther will create a wolf howl with Thaumaturgy.

They consider attacking from several sides, then settle on staying together
near the rear of the caravan. They hope to separate the rear from the front,
force the guards to chase scattered animals, and take supplies without
fighting everyone. Even capturing a single loaded camel would be worthwhile.

The party takes a position on a rocky ledge beside the caravan's route.

## 13:00–18:00 — spotted before the ambush

Closer observation shows alert, rested guards with good equipment. The party's
stealth is insufficient: a guard notices them and shouts before their planned
trick can begin. Initiative is rolled; they do not get the surprise they hoped
for. Prim acts first.

The ledge is established as ten feet high, with approaches at its sides. The

of the discussion about whether the ledge makes the party safe is player

different directions.** Guards farther from the party go after the animals,

Gunther tells the guards that wolves are chasing them and that they are lost

exhaustion penalties to **16**. He does not threaten the guards.

Hungry companions urge the party to accept. Some players discuss feigning
cooperation and attacking at close range, but no such attack is carried out.
The group's willingness to fight collapses as companions decide to stand down.
